Data notes and method

So that any reviewer can verify these charts against the source, this section sets out every cleaning and exclusion decision, and offers the full raw rows for download.

Source files

DF_DMM2_Extracted_Data_from_Photo.xlsx, 355 product lines from the DF Lagardère shop at King Fahd International Airport, Dammam (DMM2, Gate 20), photographed on 26 May 2026. KFIA_JDF_Price_Comparison.xlsx (from Alexander), 52 reference products from Jeddah (JEDA), each checked for presence and price at DMM2. All prices are in SAR.

Prices

A price was treated as readable only where the cell held a plain number. 301 of 355 lines were priced; 54 were not (for example ‘Not visible’) and are excluded from price charts but retained in the assortment counts.

Category consolidation

Categories use the top-level token of the recorded category. Only singular and plural variants were merged: Souvenir into Souvenirs, Toy into Toys, and Fragrance into Fragrances. No other re-classification was applied, which leaves 21 categories.

Charts with a minimum sample

Box plots are shown only for categories with at least five priced lines; median by category is shown for categories with at least four priced lines. Smaller categories are still counted in the assortment chart.

Promotions

Each promoted line is assigned to exactly one mechanic, by priority (diplomatic price, then buy X get Y, then multibuy or mix, then per cent off or save, then other offer), so the mechanic counts sum to the promotion total of 85.
